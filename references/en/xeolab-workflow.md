# XeoLab client workflow

Apply this workflow alongside the existing SEO, AEO, GEO, LLMO, Naver, content and measurement checks; its approval requirements take precedence.

## 1. Scope and baseline

Confirm the client's domain, goals, markets and languages, access, scope and constraints. Inspect public pages, supplied data and authorized tools read-only. Record collection time, URLs, samples, period, environment and baselines. Mark inaccessible information as unverified and omit account secrets from reports.

## 2. Audit and recommendations

Assess the five lanes and content operations, distinguishing observations from assumptions. Report every finding in the format below. Base severity on impact, scope, urgency and confidence; estimate scope by file, page, account and work unit, identifying uncertain estimates.

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Problem | Observed issue and impact |
| Evidence | URL, material, observation date, reproduction and unknowns |
| Severity | High, medium or low with rationale |
| Recommendation | Options, hypothesized benefit and limitations |
| Estimated scope | Targets, changes and estimated work units |
| Approval required | Whether required, pending or approved and approved scope |
| Follow-up metrics | Baseline, source, timing and comparison method |

## 3. Approval and execution

Before site or code changes, deployments, publishing, editing or deleting content, external submissions or account settings changes, present targets, specific work and impact and obtain explicit user approval. A request for an audit or draft is not approval. Before approval provide only read-only diagnosis and unpublished drafts. Obtain renewed approval for scope changes and record approval and results. Confirm the recipient and content before externally transmitting secrets or client data.

## 4. Verification and measurement

Record before-and-after changes and crawler-facing verification for approved work. Propose follow-up measurement with baselines, sources, timing and limitations for available indicators such as impressions, clicks, indexing and AI citations. Separate results from external factors; never guarantee AI visibility, citations, rankings or revenue growth or assert causality without evidence.
